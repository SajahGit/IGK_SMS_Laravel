1. Executive System Blueprint & Modular Topology

The IGK School Management System (Laravel 12.71.0) is an enterprise-grade multi-campus educational resource planning platform. The entire architecture is organized into 6 Functional Domains, serving 8 User Portals, powered by 25 Core Functional Modules (encompassing 35 specialized sub-systems) and governed by 71 Dedicated Business Logic & Calculation Engines.
