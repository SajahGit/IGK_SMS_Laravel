# Teacher guide

Sign in with a **Teacher** account; you land on `/teacher/dashboard`. You only see classes and subjects assigned to you.

## Attendance
1. Open **Attendance** and pick the class and date.
2. Mark each student Present, Absent, Late or Excused; add a remark if needed.
3. Save. Sessions stay editable only inside the configured edit window; after that ask an Admin.

## Marks
1. Open **Exams**, choose the exam and your class-subject.
2. Enter marks for the whole class in one sheet; tick **Absent** instead of typing 0 for absentees.
3. Save. The whole sheet is saved atomically. Marks lock once results are published.

## Timetable
**Timetable** shows your weekly slots (read-only).
