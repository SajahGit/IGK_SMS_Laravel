# Student guide

Sign in with the account the school created for you. You must change the first password on first login.

- **Results**: only published results appear; open one to download the report card PDF.
- **Attendance**: your attendance by subject and date.
- **Invoices**: fees due, payments made and the remaining balance; download the invoice PDF.
- **Timetable**: your class's weekly schedule.
- **Profile**: your details (contact the registrar to change official data).
